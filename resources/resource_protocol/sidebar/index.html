Sidebar

Ctrl+Alt+C also toggles it. Touch the window's left edge to show the sidebar.

On startup

Restored tabs load when you click them. Ctrl+Shift+T reopens a closed tab.

New tab opens

With the address bar, Ctrl+T and New Tab show a floating address bar over the page, like Zen. A tab is only created when you pick something.

Unload inactive tabs

Frees the memory of tabs you haven't used for a while. They stay in the sidebar and reload when clicked. Tabs playing audio or video are kept.

Ad blocking

Uses uBlock Origin's filter lists. Turn it off for one site with the shield in the address bar.

Passwords

Stillwater offers to save a password when you sign in. Passwords are encrypted with your Windows account.

History and bookmarks

Ctrl+H shows your history and Ctrl+D bookmarks the current page. Visits are kept on this computer for 90 days.
